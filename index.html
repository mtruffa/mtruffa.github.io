Jarvis

A personal assistant, used only by its owner.

Jarvis runs on its owner's own computer. It answers questions about the owner's notes and, with the owner's permission, reads their Google Calendar and Gmail so it can say what's coming up, summarise unread mail, add or move calendar events, and prepare email drafts for the owner to review.

What it can't do

Jarvis never sends email. It can only create drafts, which the owner reads and sends themselves (or deletes). It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and has no accounts to sign up for.
